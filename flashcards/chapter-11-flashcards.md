## Chapter 11: Tracing Funds End to End, Wallet Risk Scoring, and Documenting Findings — Flashcards

| # | Front (Question) | Back (Answer) |
|---|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | SAR filing deadline for a depository institution when a suspect IS identified? | 30 calendar days from date of initial determination. Exam tip: extends to 60 days total only if NO suspect is identified. |
| 2 | How long must SARs and supporting documentation be retained? | 5 years from the date of filing. Exam tip: applies to the SAR itself plus all supporting workpapers/evidence. |
| 3 | OFAC blocking report deadline after freezing sanctioned funds? | Within 10 business days of the blocking action. Exam tip: separate obligation from SAR filing — both may be required simultaneously. |
| 4 | BSA SAR dollar thresholds for depository institutions? | $5,000 if a suspect can be identified; $25,000 if no suspect can be identified. Exam tip: MSBs use a lower $2,000 threshold. |
| 5 | Direct vs. indirect wallet exposure — how do they differ in scoring? | Direct = wallet transacted straight with the illicit address (highest weight). Indirect = exposure arrives through intermediary hops (weighted lower, decays with distance, but still material). |
| 6 | Sanctions vs. darknet vs. mixer exposure — how are they combined? | Each is scored independently based on volume %, hop distance, and recency, then aggregated into a single composite wallet risk score. |
| 7 | Standard investigation lifecycle order (lead to conclusion)? | Lead intake → Scoping → Fund-flow tracing → Risk scoring → Documentation → Disposition (file/no-file SAR) → Law enforcement referral if warranted. |
| 8 | Core elements required for chain-of-custody in a blockchain case file? | Transaction hashes, timestamped screenshots, hash-verified data exports, documented tracing methodology, and analyst attestation. |
| 9 | What must a compliant fund-flow narrative capture (the '5 W's')? | Who, What, When, Where, and Why the activity is suspicious — presented as a chronological, hop-by-hop trace with addresses and amounts. |
| 10 | Structure of a hop-by-hop fund-flow entry in a case file? | Source address → destination address → amount/asset → timestamp/tx hash → risk factor observed at that hop. Repeat per hop until disposition. |
| 11 | Key handoff points between compliance and law enforcement? | Grand jury subpoena receipt, law enforcement request for underlying records, and formal SAR-sharing under the safe-harbor provision — compliance may share but the SAR itself remains confidential. |
| 12 | SAR confidentiality rule — what is prohibited? | Tipping off: disclosing to the subject of a SAR (or unauthorized parties) that a SAR has been filed is prohibited under the SAR confidentiality provision. Exam tip: this applies even after account closure. |
