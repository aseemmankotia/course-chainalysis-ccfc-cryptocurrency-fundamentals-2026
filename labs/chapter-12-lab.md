# Chapter 12 Lab: Full CCFC Exam Simulation and Time-Management Strategy

**Estimated time:** 45 minutes

## Exercise

Complete the full timed practice simulation (all six domains) under exam-like conditions, then self-grade and build a two-day focused review plan targeting your lowest-scoring domain

## Key takeaway

Exam-day performance depends as much on time-management and review strategy as it does on domain knowledge, so rehearse both together before the real assessment.
