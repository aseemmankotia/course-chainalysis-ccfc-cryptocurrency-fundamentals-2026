# Chapter 6 Lab: Following the Flow of Funds: Pseudonymity vs Anonymity

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Trace a sample multi-hop transaction path across three or four addresses on a block explorer and diagram direct vs indirect exposure to a labeled risky address

## Key takeaway

Flow-of-funds tracing follows value across hops and chains to establish exposure, even when full real-world identity remains unresolved.
