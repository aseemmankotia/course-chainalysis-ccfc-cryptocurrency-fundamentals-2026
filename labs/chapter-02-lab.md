# Chapter 2 Lab: Bitcoin, Ethereum, Stablecoins, Tokens, and the Crypto Ecosystem

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Look up a well-known ERC-20 token contract on Etherscan and distinguish the token contract address from a user wallet address

## Key takeaway

Knowing whether an asset is a native coin or a token, and which ecosystem participant handled it, is the first triage step in almost every compliance and investigative scenario.
