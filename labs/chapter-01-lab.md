# Chapter 1 Lab: Blockchain 101: Distributed Ledgers, Blocks, and Consensus

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Explore a live Bitcoin block on a public block explorer (e.g., mempool.space or Blockstream.info) and identify block height, hash, previous hash, nonce, and transaction count

## Key takeaway

A blockchain is a shared, append-only ledger secured by consensus, not by anonymity, and every transaction is visible even though addresses aren't inherently identified.
