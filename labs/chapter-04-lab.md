# Chapter 4 Lab: Building, Signing, Broadcasting a Transaction, and Wallet Custody Models

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Trace a real historical transaction on a public block explorer, identifying fee paid, number of confirmations, and whether it moved into a custodial exchange deposit address

## Key takeaway

Compliance teams must evaluate both where a wallet sits in the transaction lifecycle and who actually controls its private keys before assessing risk.
