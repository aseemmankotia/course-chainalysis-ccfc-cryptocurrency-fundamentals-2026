# Chapter 3 Lab: Public/Private Keys, Address Formats, and the UTXO vs Account Models

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Generate a test keypair with a testnet wallet tool and identify the public key, private key, and derived address; compare a Bitcoin Bech32 address to an Ethereum address

## Key takeaway

Every transaction-tracing exercise starts with correctly reading whether the chain uses UTXOs or account balances, since that determines how fund flows are followed.
