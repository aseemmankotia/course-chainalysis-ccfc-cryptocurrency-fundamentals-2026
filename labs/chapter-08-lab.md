# Chapter 8 Lab: KYC/CDD, Know Your Transaction (KYT), and the Regulatory Landscape

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Build a simple KYT-style risk-scoring checklist (counterparty type, geography, transaction size, direct exposure to sanctioned entity) and score three sample transactions

## Key takeaway

KYC establishes who a customer is at onboarding; KYT continuously monitors what they do afterward, and both feed the same risk-based compliance program.
