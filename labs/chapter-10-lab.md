# Chapter 10 Lab: Stolen Funds, Hacks, Sanctions Evasion, and Money-Laundering Patterns

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Reconstruct a simplified post-hack fund flow (exploit address to mixer to multiple exchange deposit addresses) and mark each stage as placement, layering, or integration

## Key takeaway

Recognizing which money-laundering stage a fund flow is in — placement, layering, or integration — tells investigators where in the chain intervention is still possible.
