# Chapter 11 Lab: Tracing Funds End to End, Wallet Risk Scoring, and Documenting Findings

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Draft a one-page investigative summary for a sample case: fund-flow diagram, risk score rationale, and recommended next action (file SAR, escalate, or close)

## Key takeaway

A finished investigation is only as strong as its documentation, since every risk score and fund-flow claim must be traceable back to specific on-chain evidence.
