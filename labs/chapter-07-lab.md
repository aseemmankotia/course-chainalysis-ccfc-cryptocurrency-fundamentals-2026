# Chapter 7 Lab: AML/CFT Obligations, VASP Requirements, and the FATF Travel Rule

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Map a sample cross-exchange transfer scenario to the required Travel Rule data fields (originator name, account, beneficiary name, account) and identify what's missing

## Key takeaway

AML/CFT frameworks like the FATF Travel Rule push VASPs to share sender and recipient information, extending traditional wire-transfer rules into crypto.
