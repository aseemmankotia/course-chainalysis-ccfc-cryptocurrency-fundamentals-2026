# Chapter 9 Lab: Scams, Ransomware, and Darknet Markets

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Given three anonymized on-chain fund-flow diagrams, classify each as rug pull, ransomware payment, or darknet market payout based on structural indicators (liquidity pool drain, single ransom address fan-out, escrow release pattern)

## Key takeaway

Each illicit typology leaves a distinct on-chain fund-flow signature that analysts learn to recognize before confirming with off-chain evidence.
